HOW TO PLAY: PORCH OF A DEAL

  • SPIN THE WHEEL: The wheel randomly selects a family member.
  • ANSWER THE QUESTION: The host reads the trivia question and options aloud.
  • MAKE A DEAL: Get it right and pick one of the 20 doors!
  • PRIZES: 10 doors hide GREAT prizes, 10 hide GAG gifts. Choose wisely!
  • ZONK: Wrong answer? You're ZONKED! The question goes to the penalty box for 3 rounds.

HOW TO PLAY: FAMILY REOKE

  • PULL THE LEVER: The slot machine picks the Singer, Type (Solo/Duet/Group), and Song!
  • DUETS & GROUPS: If Duet or Group lands, the machine spins again for a partner!
  • WILDCARD: The rare [VOLUNTEER] wildcard lets anyone jump up and pick their own song!
  • HIT THE STAGE: The karaoke video loads right on screen — grab the mic and sing!
