How to play Porch of a Deal

1. The host reads the question out loud.
2. The player answers, then the host taps Reveal Answer.
3. Host marks it right or wrong.
4. Get it right and you pick any unopened door.
5. Behind it: a real prize, or a gag gift!
6. Miss it and you're zonked — no door, next player up.
Questions include family history, pop culture, photo rounds and physical challenges — and they reshuffle every single game.
Let's make a deal!
☎

How to play The Porch Banker

1. Answer questions to build up a pot.
2. Each correct answer climbs the money ladder.
3. Every 3rd correct answer, the Banker calls with an offer.
4. Take the DEAL and it's banked for good.
5. Say NO DEAL and keep climbing — but one wrong answer wipes the un-banked pot.
Same question bank as Porch of a Deal — family history, pop culture, photos and challenges — reshuffled each game.
