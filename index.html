📜 Our Family History
From Benin to Virginia, from Georgia to today

The Porch Family's story stretches from the culturally rich heritage of Benin, Nigeria and the people of the Fulani tribe to its roots in America. While our lineage can be traced as far back as Virginia through Thomas and James Porch, our family legacy here is most fully celebrated through Frank Porch (1870s–1950s) and Katherine "Carrie" Hall (1880–c.1913) of Pike County, Georgia. Together they built the foundation of the generations we honor today.

Pre-Colonization · Benin & Nigeria

Our ancestors lived in the land of Benin and among the Fulani tribe, where they served as chiefs, queens, warriors, and healers. Through the transatlantic slave trade, our family line was carried to America.

1700s · Virginia & North Carolina

1749 — James Porch is born in the Colony of Virginia, one of the earliest known Porch ancestors.
1754 — Hugh Hall is born in Ireland; he later marries Mary Reid (b.1761, NC).
1772 — Thomas Porch, son of James, is born in Virginia.
1786 — Alexander Hall, son of Hugh and Mary, is born in NC. He will later migrate to Georgia.

1800s · Georgia

1800 — Henley Jones Sammons is born; he later fathers children with Philis Sammons (b.1809), a woman enslaved in Georgia. Together they raise Violet Sammons.
1801 — Elizabeth Brown is born; she will later marry Alexander Hall.
1823 — John Thomas Hall, son of Alexander & Elizabeth, is born.
1833 — Jerry Porch is born in Virginia. He later migrates to Georgia and marries Jane Dorsey (b.1836, GA).
1840 — Violet Sammons is born to Henley and Philis.
1846 — William "Pop Bill" Hall is born, son of John Thomas Hall & Martha Jones Sammons.
1853 — Frank Porch Sr. is born in Monroe County, son of Jerry & Jane Dorsey.
1870s — Frank Porch Jr. is born, son of Frank Sr.
1880 — Katherine "Carrie" Hall is born, daughter of William "Pop Bill" Hall & Violet Sammons.
Late 1800s–Early 1900s — Frank Porch marries Katherine Hall. They raise five children: Mattie, Frankie Lee, Lucy, Alberta, and James.

1900s · Tennessee, Georgia, Texas, Illinois

Their children establish new branches: the Morelands, Dennises, Hartsfields, Walkers, and the continuing Porch line. The family legacy spreads from Georgia into new states and new generations.

2000s–Today

From Benin to Virginia, from Tennessee & Illinois to Georgia, from Frank & Katherine's porch to ours today — the family story is one of endurance, resilience, and love.
